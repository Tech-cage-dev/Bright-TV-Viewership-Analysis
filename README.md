# 📺 Bright TV Viewership Analysis
## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses **Bright TV viewership data** to understand audience behaviour, viewing patterns, customer segments, and the key factors that influence engagement.

The objective was to transform raw viewership data into actionable insights that could help Bright TV better understand its audience, identify high-value customer segments, and support data-driven programming and business decisions.

The project combines **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, SQL/Python analysis, and dashboard development** to provide a comprehensive view of viewer behaviour.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focused on answering the following key questions:

- Who are Bright TV's viewers?
- What are the most popular programmes and viewing categories?
- When are viewers most active?
- Which customer segments generate the highest engagement?
- What factors are associated with increased viewing activity?
- Are there identifiable viewing patterns across different audience groups?
- How can viewer insights support better content and customer strategies?

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The raw dataset was prepared for analysis by:

- Checking for missing values
- Identifying and handling duplicate records
- Standardising data formats
- Cleaning categorical variables
- Validating numerical fields
- Creating analytical variables
- Preparing data for segmentation and visualisation

Data preparation was an important step to ensure that the analysis was based on consistent and reliable information.

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

The exploratory analysis investigated patterns across:

- Viewer demographics
- Viewing duration
- Programme/category preferences
- Viewing frequency
- Viewing times
- Customer engagement
- Subscription/viewer characteristics
- Other relevant behavioural indicators

The analysis was used to identify trends, anomalies, and relationships within the dataset.

---

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

A key component of the project was **viewer segmentation**.

Viewers were grouped according to behavioural and/or demographic characteristics to identify distinct audience profiles.

This helps answer questions such as:

- Which viewers are highly engaged?
- Which viewers have lower viewing activity?
- Which audience groups have similar viewing behaviour?
- What types of content are preferred by different segments?

Segmentation provides a foundation for more targeted content, engagement, and customer strategies.

---

## 📊 Key Drivers of Viewership

The analysis also explored potential factors influencing viewer engagement.

Examples include:

- Programme/category
- Viewing time
- Viewer demographics
- Viewing frequency
- Duration of viewing
- Customer characteristics
- Content preferences

The purpose was not only to describe what happened, but to investigate **why certain viewing patterns occurred**.
